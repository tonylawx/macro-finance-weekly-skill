# Event Calendar & Earnings Radar Guide

Core data collection, calendar structuring, and evaluation standards for the Monday Macro & US Equities Weekly Wrap.

---

## 1. Macro Indicators Priority Hierarchy

### Tier 1: Valuation Anchor & Policy Trajectory
Primary economic releases that directly shift benchmark discount rates, market cost of capital, and Federal Reserve policy expectations:
- **CPI (Consumer Price Index) / Core CPI**: Headline vs. supercore dynamics, shelter inflation lag, and transport services.
- **PCE (Personal Consumption Expenditures) / Core PCE**: The Federal Reserve's preferred inflation gauge; real consumption expenditure vs. headline deflator.
- **Non-Farm Payrolls (NFP) & Unemployment Rate**: Household vs. establishment survey discrepancies, average hourly earnings (wage-push inflation), and labor force participation rate.
- **FOMC Interest Rate Decision, Dot Plot & Press Conference**: Target range adjustments, terminal rate shifts, economic projections summary (SEP), and Powell's forward guidance.
- **Treasury Quarterly Refunding Announcement (QRA)**: Borrowing estimates, issuance split between short-term bills and long-dated coupons, and primary dealer absorption capacity.

### Tier 2: Growth Momentum & Cyclical Verification
Secondary economic releases verifying economic health, consumption resilience, or impending contraction risks:
- **ISM Manufacturing & Services PMI**: New orders, supplier deliveries, and prices paid sub-indices.
- **Retail Sales (MoM / YoY)**: Control group spending verifying consumer resilience against credit tightening.
- **Initial & Continuing Jobless Claims**: High-frequency weekly gauge of labor market inflection points.
- **Gross Domestic Product (GDP)**: Advance, second, and third estimates; parsing private domestic final purchases vs. inventory/trade distortions.

## 2. Earnings Radar & Implied Move Tracking

### Bellwethers by Sector
1. **Mega-Cap Tech & Semiconductors**:
   - Tickers: `**NVDA**`, `**MSFT**`, `**AAPL**`, `**GOOGL**`, `**AMZN**`, `**META**`, `**TSM**`, `**AMD**`.
   - Core Audit: Hyperscaler capital expenditure (CapEx) commitments vs. commercialized enterprise cloud revenues; free cash flow yields; hardware delivery lead times.
2. **Industrial & Consumer Barometers**:
   - Tickers: `**CAT**`, `**WMT**`, `**COST**`, `**UNP**`.
   - Core Audit: Backlog fulfillment, inventory turnover, freight rates, and lower-to-middle income consumer wallet strain.
3. **Financials & Capital Markets**:
   - Tickers: `**JPM**`, `**GS**`, `**MS**`, `**BAC**`.
   - Core Audit: Net interest margin (NIM) trajectory, credit loss provisions (allowances for bad debt), and investment banking underwriting pipeline recovery.

### Mandatory Tracking Metrics for Upcoming Releases
- **Consensus Revenue & Adjusted EPS**: Street expectations vs. whisper numbers.
- **Options Market Implied Move ($\pm\%$)**: Implied single-day post-earnings straddle pricing compared against trailing 8-quarter average actual moves to detect pricing dislocations.
- **Forward Guidance Revisions**: Next-quarter and full-year guidance deltas regarding gross margin expansion, operating expenses, and cash flow durability.
